# Legal Disclaimer

**Read this before using FED-SPA for anything that matters.**

## What FED-SPA is

FED-SPA is a convenience layer over public records. The licensed tier
lists massage establishments licensed by the Florida Department of
Health, Board of Massage Therapy, as returned by the MQA verification
portal. The unlicensed tier (subscriber-only) lists businesses we could
not match to a license — with the reason.

## What FED-SPA is not

- **Not a government service.** We are not affiliated with, endorsed by,
  or connected to the Florida DOH, the Board of Massage Therapy, or MQA.
- **Not a live feed.** Data is a snapshot, refreshed **once a year**
  (see the "Data as of" chip in every surface). A license can be
  suspended, revoked, or issued the day after our snapshot.
- **Not a lawyer, cop, or inspector.** "No license found" in the
  subscriber tier means *we* could not find one in *our* snapshot. It is
  not a legal determination that a business is operating unlawfully.
- **Not a review site.** Presence on the licensed list is not an
  endorsement — of quality, safety, or anything else.

## The authoritative source is always the portal

Before relying on any record here, verify it yourself:

**https://mqa-internet.doh.state.fl.us/MQASearchServices/HealthCareProviders**

License status on this list is a snapshot, not a live feed — always
re-verify a specific license on the official portal before relying on it.
That link is one tap away in every FED-SPA surface (the "Verify it
yourself" section on web, the About card in the extension options, the
settings screens on mobile).

## Accuracy and corrections

We verify records manually at refresh time and record `data_as_of` and
`last_checked` per row. Errors still happen. File a *Data correction*
issue with what the portal shows and the date you checked; fixes ship
with the next annual refresh (or a hotfix release for serious errors).

## Subscriber tier content

The unlicensed watchlist contains **observations, not accusations**.
Reasons are factual ("no license found in MQA under this business name
as of <date>"). Businesses can be licensed under different names, at
different addresses, or after our snapshot date. If you are a business
owner listed in the subscriber tier and believe it's in error, file a
data correction with your MQA record and it will be handled with
priority.

## No warranty

FED-SPA is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or
implied. Use it as a starting point for your own verification, never as
the final word.

## Privacy

There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no backend. The
apps read bundled files; the subscriber code, when stored, stays on the
device. We collect nothing because there is nothing to collect with.
