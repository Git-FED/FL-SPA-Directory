## The upstream authors

FED-SPA organizes public records; it does not originate them. The license facts themselves belong to the State of Florida's Medical Quality Assurance program and are observed via the public portal (https://mqa-internet.doh.state.fl.us/MQASearchServices/HealthCareProviders). The project's [CITATIONS.md](CITATIONS.md) defines how downstream users should credit *us*; this section is how *we* credit the source: the MQA portal's public data is the foundation everything here is built on.

## Attribution requirements (when you fork)

Per [LICENSE](LICENSE) (code, MIT) and [COPYING.md](COPYING.md) (dataset, ODbL), forks and derivatives carry this notice forward — the recommended form is in [CITATIONS.md](CITATIONS.md). Keeping this AUTHORS page (or its equivalent) intact in derivative works is part of honest attribution, and it's cheap.

## Corrections to this page

Credit mistakes are real bugs: open an issue or PR and it gets fixed without ceremony. Someone missing who should be here is a defect; someone listed who asked not to be is a privacy defect — both fix on sight.
