# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

FED-SPA exists so that anyone can quickly check whether a Florida massage establishment holds a license. The people who make it — maintainers, contributors, reporters of data corrections, and participants in Discussions — deserve to do that work in a space that is decent, honest, and free of harassment. This code sets the floor for behavior in every FED-SPA space: the issue tracker, pull requests, Discussions, the wiki, and any channel a maintainer identifies as project space.

## Our standards

**Behavior that keeps the project healthy:**

- Focusing on the record, the code, and the evidence. The project's core habit is "what did the portal show, on what date?" — carry that habit into disagreements.
- Being precise about uncertainty. "No license found in a search on 2026-09-07" is the project's house style because it's honest; use it everywhere.
- Respecting the three constraints (no backend, no third-party, annual cadence) even when arguing against them — argue with the design, don't smuggle around it.
- Accepting correction gracefully, especially on data. Every maintainer has shipped a typo; every contributor has misread a portal page.
- Showing empathy for people whose experience differs from yours.

**Behavior that gets you removed:**

- Harassment of any kind: comments about or targeting gender, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, race, religion, national origin, or any protected characteristic.
- Sexual attention or imagery of any kind. Given the subject matter of this directory, we state this plainly: discussion of establishments as *businesses with licenses* is the project's entire scope, and anything sexualized about them, their workers, or their patrons is off-topic and unwelcome.
- Targeting, doxxing, or organizing campaigns against any listed establishment, its owners, or its workers. The watchlist exists to inform, never to arm harassment — see the [discussions welcome page](discussion/welcome.md) ground rules.
- Publishing subscriber-only content in public spaces, including the shared code or decrypted watchlist records.
- Trolling, insults, or sustained bad-faith argument — including re-litigating the three constraints after being answered, in bad faith.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider unwelcome in professional settings.

## Our responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and for taking appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any instance of unacceptable behavior — including banning tempora­rily or permanently from project spaces.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, edits to the wiki, and other contributions that are not aligned with this code, and will explain the moderation reason when doing so.

## Scope

This code applies within all project spaces and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces. Representation of the project — for example, posting as a maintainer in a forum about Florida business regulation — means you are the project's face in that conversation and this code travels with you.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub's private reporting features on the repository, or by contacting a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile. If the matter involves a maintainer, report it to a *different* maintainer — see [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) for who that is. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All maintainers are obligated to respect the reporter's privacy and safety. Reports are confidential to the handling maintainer(s).

Enforcement may include: a warning, deletion of content, temporary ban, or permanent ban from all project spaces, proportional to the offense and the user's history. Doxxing, threats, and harassment campaigns are permanent-ban offenses on first instance, with no warning step.
